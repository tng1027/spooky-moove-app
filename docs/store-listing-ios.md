**Description**
```
Spooky Moove is a training companion for Chess and Xiangqi (Chinese Chess) played on a real, physical board.

Copy each move onto the simple board on your phone with two taps. When it's your turn, Spooky Moove suggests a move, highlights it on the board and shows how the game is going. You decide whether to play it.

PLAY TOGETHER, LEARN TOGETHER
• Seven levels change only the move suggested to you, from deliberately weak to full strength.
• Playing with a child? Pick a gentle level and keep every game fun and close.
• 50/50 keeps the game balanced: it eases up when you lead and plays its best when you fall behind.
• Change the level at any time during the game.

NO NOTATION NEEDED
• Moves are shown as "from ➔ to" (for example E2 ➔ E4), highlighted in green on the board.
• Castling, en passant and promotion are explained in plain words and pictures.
• Only legal moves can be entered, so the phone always matches your board.
• "I played it" records the suggested move in one tap. Played something else? Just enter it.
• Unlimited undo.

TWO GAMES IN ONE APP
• Chess with full rules: castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate, stalemate and draws.
• Xiangqi on a traditional board with river, palace and Chinese-character pieces.

FAIR AND PRIVATE
• Made for training, study, handicap games and friendly play — not for rated or tournament games. A fair-play notice explains this on first launch.
• Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.
• English and Vietnamese.

OPEN SOURCE
Spooky Moove is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later and uses the open-source Fairy-Stockfish engine (GPLv3). Source code and licenses are available from Settings → About & Licenses.
```

## Review Notes (App Review Information → Notes, English)

```
Thank you for reviewing Spooky Moove.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Spooky Moove is a training companion for Chess and Xiangqi (Chinese Chess) played on a physical board. The user copies each move from their real board onto the phone (two taps). On the user's turn, the app suggests a move at the chosen level. The app never plays against the user and never moves pieces on its own; the user always enters every move.

NO ACCOUNT, NO NETWORK
No sign-in or demo account is needed. The app works fully offline: all analysis runs on the device. It has no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and collects no data.

HOW TO TEST (about 1 minute)
1. On first launch, read the fair-play notice and tap the button to continue.
2. On the home screen, tap CHESS (or XIANGQI).
3. Keep the defaults (level 50/50, White) and tap START GAME.
4. A suggested move appears (e.g. E2 ➔ E4), highlighted in green. Tap "I PLAYED IT" to record it.
5. Enter the opponent's reply: tap a black piece, then tap its destination.
6. The next suggestion appears. Try another level from the level row, UNDO, and NEW GAME.
Language: Home → language button (top right) → English / Tiếng Việt.

FAIR PLAY
The app is positioned for training, study, handicap games (e.g. a parent playing with a child) and friendly play. A one-time fair-play notice on first launch states that it must not be used in rated, sanctioned or tournament games. It can be viewed again from the FAIR PLAY button on the new-game screen.

- Engine: https://github.com/tng1027/Fairy-Stockfish
License texts are available in the app: Home → Settings (top left) → ABOUT & LICENSES.
```
